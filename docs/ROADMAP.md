# LessonPress school-readiness roadmap

## 1. Exam documents

Delivered in this increment: school logo embedded in the paper; section headings/instructions at question boundaries; individual question marks with validated marking totals; summed document totals; page-number print margin boxes. Existing illustration, image upload and page-break controls remain.

Print margin boxes require browser support (Chrome 131+); inspect print preview and disable the browser's own headers/footers to avoid duplicates. Physical printer and real-phone trials remain outstanding. No claim of a full diagram-authoring canvas.

## 2. Reusable question bank

Delivered: personal browser bank, up to 50 source-linked questions, subject/class/topic/term search, exact visuals and marking keys, bounded JSON backup import/export, and assembly of 3–20 questions from the same subject/class into a fresh unreviewed device paper. Sections and page breaks are deliberately arranged anew.

This is not cloud sync or a shared school question bank. Browser storage can be cleared; export backups. Shared reuse is now available separately in School question bank, derived from approved versions under existing school/department access. It retains source attribution and creates a fresh unapproved draft.

## 3. School review

Existing: question-number feedback, independent approval, immutable revisions and approved-copy preservation. Delivered: accessible side-by-side revision comparison, feedback beside each question, tab-local version-specific reviewer progress, approved-copy links/print stamps and approved-source school question reuse. Next: a second teacher completing the full workflow independently, and deciding whether reviewers need progress synced across devices. Do not loosen existing school access or self-approval checks.

## 4. Classroom trials

Use Kindergarten, Primary and SS1 Chemistry papers. A second teacher should independently prepare/review/print each. Record time, confusing controls, missing content, phone usability and print defects. Synthetic verification cannot substitute for this evidence.

## 5. Small school pilot

Before wider confidential-exam use: privacy/access and recovery review, onboarding, support route, export/recovery drill, usage limits and outcome measurement (completed usable papers and returning teachers). No fabricated pilot results or approvals.

Walkthrough video is deferred until the core workflow is stable.
