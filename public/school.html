Need a quick guide to these words?
School workspace
Your school’s shared area. Switching schools changes the papers and colleagues you can see.
Department
A teaching team, such as Science or Primary school. The principal creates it, then assigns colleagues to it.
Draft
A paper that the author is still preparing. Save your changes before asking for a review.
Submitted / waiting for review
The author has asked another person to check the paper. Editing pauses until the reviewer requests changes or approves it.
Changes requested
The reviewer has explained what needs improving. The author edits, saves a new version and submits again.
Approved copy
A specific version checked by someone other than its author. Colleagues in the department can read and reuse it. Approval does not guarantee every fact is correct.
Version
A saved snapshot. Editing an approved paper creates a new draft while keeping the approved copy.
